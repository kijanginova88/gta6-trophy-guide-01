Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours, with one playthrough and no online trophies. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story (60–80 hours). Finish all five chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals as you travel, meditate at every spot you find, and try each activity once. The only trophy reported as possibly missable is Positive Mental Attitude: its Chapter 1 meditation spot can be lost after that chapter.
  2. Step 2 — Strangers and side strands. Finish the eight stranger mission strands, including the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and hunt Bret’s unique animal.
  3. Step 3 — Activities and challenges. 250 stunts, 20 different race wins, drone races at all 14 locations, gold at all 42 shooting ranges, and 50 of the 80 challenges (70 for the 100% checklist).
  4. Step 4 — Collectibles and the map. 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 3 card sets, every meditation spot, and every region of Leonida explored.
  5. Step 5 — Robberies, money and 100%. Five bank vaults, an armored truck, a jewelry store unseen, one illegal operation per gang, $250,000 laundered and $100,000 spent. Then finish the 100% checklist for A Long Road and the platinum pops.

Do not use cheats during a trophy run: PowerPyx reports that turning on a cheat disables trophies, and you cannot save once one is used. Cheats only unlock after the story anyway.
